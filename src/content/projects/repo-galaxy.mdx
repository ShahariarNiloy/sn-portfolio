## Why

Opening a new codebase is like arriving in a city without a map. Repo Galaxy turns the structure into something you can **see and fly through**, then answers questions about what you are looking at.

## Planned

- Paste a GitHub URL and see the repository as a 3D galaxy.
- Click any file or folder and ask the AI what it does.
- Keep it light: fast to load, even on large repositories.
