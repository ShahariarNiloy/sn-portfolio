## Why it exists

Most online dev tools send your data to a server just to format some JSON or decode a token. That is slow and, for anything sensitive, risky. **utilyx does all of it on your machine**, so it is instant and private by design.

## How it's built

- **Zero-server architecture.** Every tool runs client-side; the site is static. [Read more](/highlights/zero-server)
- **Works offline.** Once loaded, the tools keep working without a connection, and most operations finish in under a millisecond.
- **Keyboard-first.** A ⌘K command palette jumps to any tool, and the interface is built to be accessible, with semantic markup and skip links.
- **Built with** Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
