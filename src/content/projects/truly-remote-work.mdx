## What we did

- **An automated pipeline in Go** that pulls roles from company hiring systems and screens out anything with location restrictions, so every listing is truly worldwide.
- **Programmatic SEO pages** built with Astro, so people find roles straight from search.
- **A community around it:** a weekly digest, plus Telegram and Discord groups.
- **Grew it without ads**, through LinkedIn and Facebook posts that list companies hiring remotely.
