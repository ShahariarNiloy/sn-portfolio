## The idea

Most finance content tells you what happened. GeoRenus explains **why it happened and what it means** for business and everyday decisions — with data, and with the strongest case from each side, so readers can make up their own minds.

## What we did

- **Built it end to end**: a Next.js frontend on a NestJS backend.
- **Three ways to read:** long-form articles, quick InShorts, and interactive calculators and analysers.
- **Write and edit** data-backed pieces on money, markets and power.
