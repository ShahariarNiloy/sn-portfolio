## The problem

Most of the site's pages **weren't being indexed**. Language versions competed with each other instead of pointing to each other, the sitemap listed far more URLs than really existed as distinct pages, and crawlers spent their time on slow, dynamic pages instead of the ones worth finding.

## How it was shipped

- **In phases.** One change at a time, so any shift in indexing could be traced to its cause.
- **Measured monthly.** Indexing in Search Console and Lighthouse scores for every key page template, reviewed every month with countermeasures for the next.

## Where it stands

Indexed pages **climbed steadily, month after month**. It isn't finished: some language duplicates are still indexed separately, and the next round of canonical work targets them.

## What I took from it

**Search engines reward clarity.** One page, one URL, one canonical — and a sitemap that tells the truth.
