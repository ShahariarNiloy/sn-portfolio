## In short

Bangla script has many similar-looking characters, which makes handwriting hard for machines to read. We built a **CNN-based model** that takes an image of a single handwritten character and recognises it, across **73 classes**: 50 basic letters, 10 digits and 13 special characters.

It was trained and tested on **24,231 images** from the CMATERdb dataset. The paper's next step was a full Bangla handwriting recognition system.

## What carried over

Research taught me not to trust a number I can't reproduce. An accuracy figure means little without data the model never saw, and a speed-up means little without a before and after measured the same way. That habit came with me into software engineering: the [design system](/highlights/design-system) was measured component by component, and [performance work](/highlights/performance) ships in stages so every change in a score can be traced to its cause.
