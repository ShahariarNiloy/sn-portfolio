It’s funny that…

We spend so much time trying to perfect our appearance that we sometimes forget what people actually remember.

Years from now, no one will remember whether your skin was a few shades darker, whether you had dark circles, or whether your face was perfectly symmetrical. They will remember how you made them feel.

Were you kind?\
Were you respectful?\
Did your words bring comfort?\
Were you someone people felt safe around?

There is nothing wrong with taking care of your appearance. Looking your best is a good thing. But appearance should never become the measure of your worth.

Beauty changes. Hair turns grey, skin wrinkles, and bodies age. A good heart, gentle words, honesty, patience, and good character are the things that grow more valuable with time.

Don’t spend your whole life chasing perfection in the mirror while neglecting the person looking back at you.

In the end, people rarely remember the face that impressed them the most. They remember the heart that gave them peace.
