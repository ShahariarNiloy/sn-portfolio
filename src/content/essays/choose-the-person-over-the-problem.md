Don’t let your anger win.\
Don’t let your grudges win.\
Don’t let your ego win.

Life changes in a blink, and decisions have to be made.

The people you love most, your parents, children, husband or wife, brothers, sisters, friends, the ones closest to your hearts, can leave your lives in ways you never expected. Sometimes distance takes them away. Sometimes life itself does.

We keep thinking there will always be another meeting, another conversation, another chance to sort things out.

But what if there isn’t?

Sometimes the last conversation is an argument. The last meeting feels ordinary. And only later do we realize that we were holding onto something that was never worth losing someone over.

Life is too uncertain to leave things broken with the people who matter.

Sort things out while you still can. Talk while there is still someone to talk to. Choose the person over the problem. They are your people, not strangers.

Because one day, the problem may no longer matter.\
But the person might.

And once they are gone, they may be gone for good.
