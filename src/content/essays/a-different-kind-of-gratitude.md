I think there is a different kind of gratitude that goes beyond just being thankful for what we have.

It is also about being grateful for the people Allah brings into our lives. Some become close to us, some teach us something, some become good memories, and some leave something behind that stays with us even after they are gone.

Maybe every person we meet deserves some gratitude. Not always because of what they did for us, but simply because, for some time, Allah chose to make their path cross with ours.

We may never understand why some people came into our lives, why some had to leave and why some stayed.

But maybe we don’t always need to understand everything to be grateful for it.

Some blessings come as things.\
Some come as moments.\
Some come as people.

So be grateful for everything you have, and also for everything you have lost.

Be grateful for the people who left, the people who stayed and became the reason for your smile, the moments you got to live, and even the things that didn’t work out.
