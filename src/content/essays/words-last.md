Words can last for a very, very long time.

The things people say to you can stay with you almost forever. A single expression, whether kind or cruel, can leave a lasting mark. The same is true for the words you speak to others.

Even a brief interaction with a stranger matters. Think about someone working in a cafe. The way you speak to them may influence how they feel for the rest of the day, and that feeling can ripple into how they treat their loved ones when they get home. If you make someone’s day harder, there’s a chance that frustration will be passed on in one way or another. Likewise, a small act of kindness can spread just as far.

Words have power. They can encourage, heal, inspire, or they can wound and discourage. That’s why we all should choose our words wisely and speak with kindness. Let neither our words nor our tone become the reason for our own unhappiness or someone else’s.
