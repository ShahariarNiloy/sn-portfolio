Perhaps the purpose of an argument was never to decide who was right and who won it.

Every win creates a loser, and people rarely accept losing without feeling some sense of shame. That shame often turns into defensiveness, and defensiveness slowly turns a conversation into a battle.

Sometimes, a conversation is simply a knot, formed by words, emotions, assumptions, and things left unsaid.

Pull harder, and the knot only tightens. Maybe the wiser approach is to stop pulling.

Listen. Understand. Find where the knot began, and slowly unravel it together.

Because when we enter a conversation with something to learn, rather than something to prove, there is no need for anyone to lose.

And perhaps that is the kind of conversation where both people can walk away with something more valuable than being right: understanding.
