Good motion explains a change — where a surface came from, what was selected, what was added. Bad motion just makes people wait.

## Principles

- **Purposeful.** Nothing animates for decoration.
- **Brief.** Every component transition is 300 ms or less, and exits are faster than entrances.
- **Never in the way.** Controls work during transitions, and surfaces can reverse mid-flight.
- **Spatially consistent.** Menus grow from their trigger; sheets slide in from their edge and leave the same way.
- **Never bouncy.** Curves decelerate into place without overshoot.

## Tokens, not magic numbers

Durations, easings and distances live in tokens. Components pick a role — popover, dialog, sheet — instead of raw values, so every surface of a kind moves the same way.

## Reduced motion

When someone asks for reduced motion, movement and scaling become fades while timing stays the same, so callbacks and timers still line up. Autoplay and shimmer stop; drags still follow the pointer.
