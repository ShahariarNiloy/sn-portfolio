Accessibility isn’t a sprint at the end. It’s a set of defaults that make the right thing the easy thing.

## The defaults

- **Semantic first.** Native buttons, links and inputs before ARIA; standard patterns when ARIA is needed.
- **Keyboard complete.** Everything that works with a pointer works with a keyboard.
- **Visible focus.** A clear ring, every time.
- **Never colour alone.** Status always pairs colour with text or an icon.

## Taking this site to AAA contrast

For this portfolio I checked every text colour against all three surfaces. Muted grey was the main miss: it passed AA but sat around 5.6–6.3:1. Lifting it to `#A9AEB7` brought it to 7.4–8.4:1. Score colours, success text and error text got the same treatment, and interactive targets grew to at least 44 × 44px.

<DraftNote>Brighter muted text means a little less contrast between “primary” and “secondary” text. Keep headings brightest to hold the hierarchy.</DraftNote>
