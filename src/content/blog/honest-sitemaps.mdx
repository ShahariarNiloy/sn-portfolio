A sitemap is a promise to search engines: _these are the pages worth your time_. List every URL variant you can generate and you break that promise — and crawlers spend their budget on duplicates.

## List canonical pages only

Each distinct page appears once, at its canonical URL. Language versions go inside each entry as alternates, not as separate competing URLs. On one large site, this cut the sitemap by about **98%**.

## Mind the crawl load

A bloated sitemap doesn’t just waste crawl budget — crawlers working through it can overload slow, dynamic pages. A truthful sitemap is also a performance fix.

## Test file sizes carefully

Larger sitemap files can speed up discovery, but too large raises crawl load. Change one thing at a time and watch how indexing responds.
