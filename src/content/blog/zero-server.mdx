Most online formatters, decoders and generators send whatever you paste to a server — just to format some JSON. That adds a round trip to every click, and the things developers paste are often exactly what shouldn’t leave their machine.

## Static by default

utilyx.dev is built ahead of time, so any CDN can serve it. There’s no backend to scale, patch or pay for as usage grows.

## Your device does the work

Every tool runs in the browser. Most operations finish in under a millisecond, and once a page has loaded, the tools keep working offline.

## Private by design

No uploads, no accounts, no stored input. Privacy isn’t a policy you have to trust; it’s how the thing is built.

## And it’s fast

The homepage scores **99** for performance on mobile in Lighthouse, with 100 for accessibility and best practices.

> If the data is already on the user’s machine, the work belongs there too.
